Landscape: WordPress comment spam, Isso/Cusdis no spam filter, giscus moderation in GitHub UI, Hypothesis annotations never merged back. Author-side burden: E331.

## 3. Differentiator
"Unlike Disqus, Medium and giscus, where comments sit apart from the post and moderation is either spam-flooded or done in someone else's UI, <product> filters spam automatically and turns approved inline suggestions into a credited revision of the post, for developers who keep a long-term technical blog."
